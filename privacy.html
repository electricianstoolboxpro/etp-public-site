Electrician's Toolbox Pro — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

About this application

Electrician's Toolbox Pro (ETP) is an electrical trade software project currently in development.

Google Drive backup authorization

ETP Personal Drive Backup uses Google OAuth authorization to back up development files to a Google Drive account authorized by the account owner.

The backup integration is intended for internal development use, not public customer access.

Information accessed

The backup integration requests Google Drive access to upload and verify repository backups. Authorized account credentials are used for authentication and are not published in the ETP public website or source repository.

Data storage and sharing

Backup archives are stored in the authorized Google Drive account. ETP does not sell Google user data or share it with advertisers.

Revoking access

The account owner can revoke the integration's Google account access through Google Account security settings.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to electricianstoolboxpro@gmail.com.
